# 通过快捷指令自定义 App Icon

1. ## 下载主题资源到文件应用

- 在极简桌面中，选择自己喜欢的主题点击下载。

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0020-portrait.png)

- 下载成功后会弹出分享窗口，点击**保存到文件**

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0021-portrait.png)

- 打开**文件**应用

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0022-portrait.png)

- 在**最近**中可以看到刚才下载的主题文件，点击解压

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0023-portrait.png)

1. ## 在快捷指令中设置自定义 Icon

- 打开快捷指令

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0025-portrait.png)

- 点击右上角➕号创建新的快捷指令

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0026-portrait.png)

- 在搜索操作中，搜索 **打开 App**

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0027-portrait.png)

- 点击 App，选择你要替换的 App

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0028-portrait.png)

- 点击顶部标题，重新命名，将该快捷指令明名为对应的 App 名称

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0030-portrait.png)

- 点击添加到主屏幕

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0030-portrait.png)

1. ## 添加快捷指令到屏幕

- 选择图像, **点击选取文件** 

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0031-portrait.png)

- 在弹出的窗口中，选择刚才解压的主题文件夹,在 icons 文件夹中找到喜欢的图标

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/cn/IMG_0032-portrait.png)

- 点击添加，即可完成替换

![img](https://rtquickidea.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/besttheme/TutorialPic/Resource/en/IMG_0018-portrait.png)
